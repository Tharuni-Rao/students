# Three-Minute Demo Script

1. Open the dashboard login page and switch to the administrator role.
2. Show the KPI cards and explain the synthetic student dataset is being used for demo purposes.
3. Filter by department and highlight the Student Success Score distribution and risk charts.
4. Click a student row to open the profile and explain risk triggers and interventions.
5. Use the scenario simulator to adjust attendance and coding performance and show the updated score.
6. Summarize how the platform turns raw campus signals into actionable support decisions.
